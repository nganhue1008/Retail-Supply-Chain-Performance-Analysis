# Retail-Supply-Chain-Performance-Analysis

## Problem Statement
Despite steady business operations, the company lacks a comprehensive, data-driven view of its retail performance across key areas such as sales, profitability, customer behavior, fulfillment efficiency, and product returns. Without clear visibility into these metrics, the company struggles to:

Identify which ship modes, product categories, and customer segments are most and least profitable

Understand the impact of discounts and fulfillment delays on profit margins and customer satisfaction

Detect patterns in high return rates and repeat customer behavior

Optimize strategic decisions around operations, marketing, and product delivery

### Sales and Profitability
- Sales and profit have steadily increased year-over-year, with Q4 consistently being the strongest quarter due to seasonal demand.

- Corporate and Consumer segments contribute the most to revenue, but the Consumer segment shows stronger profit margins.

- Some product subcategories (e.g., Tables, Bookcases) show high sales but low or even negative profit, suggesting pricing or cost issues.

### Ship mode and Fulfillment time
- Standard Class is the most used shipping method but has the longest fulfillment time, which may affect customer satisfaction.

- Same Day shipping, while less used, shows higher profit margins, indicating a possible premium shipping opportunity.

### Return Performance 
- The West region has the highest return rate, with Corporate customers and the Furniture category driving most returns.

- Top 3 subcategories by return rate: Tables, Binders, and Machines — suggesting potential quality, expectation mismatch, or bulk return behavior.

### Customer Loyalty
- A 98.5% repeat purchase rate indicates strong customer retention, especially within the Corporate segment.

- Home Office customers, while fewer in volume, contribute a stable base of return buyers and have the highest profit per order.

## Recommendations
- Target growth in Same Day and Second Class shipping, which yield better margins and faster delivery.

- Reassess pricing or cost structure for low-profit, high-sales products.

- Investigate return drivers in Corporate segment and implement stricter approval for bulk returns.

- Focus on loyalty programs for repeat customers, particularly in the Home Office and Consumer segments.
